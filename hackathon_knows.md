## Event (2026-10-10)

- **Dates:** October 10–12, 2026 (online). FAQ: 48-hour hackathon starting **12:30 PM IST** Saturday Oct 10.
- **Kickoff call:** 9:00 AM IST, Oct 10.
- **Web check-in opens:** 8:00 AM IST, Oct 10 (AWS credits: first 200 teams to complete check-in).
- **Hackathon / credits:** AWS and Crustdata credits transferred at **12:30 PM IST** Oct 10.
- **Submission:** Monday **12 Oct 2026, 7:00–11:55 PM IST** — public **quote post** of official neatlogs post on X (team, name, project, public repo, demo). Demo video **≤3 min**; must cover **neatlogs, Entire, cfo.ai** (architecture, implementation, runtime, reasoning, result each).
- **Winners announced:** October 16.
- **Prize pool:** $19,000 including credits ($4,000 cash + partner credits + neatlogs Pro).
- **Register:** [luma.com/s5blr882](https://luma.com/s5blr882) · **Discord:** [neatlogs.com/discord](https://neatlogs.com/discord)

## One track

**Build an agent that works (literally)** — single agent or multi-agent system; example tasks (fix GitHub issue, research with sources, watch a running system, coordinator workers, work while environment changes) are inspiration only.

**Focus areas:** planning, tool use, state, context, execution, recovery, verification, iteration.

## Required partners

Every project must use **neatlogs**, **Entire**, and **cfo.ai** with a clear role. Demo must cover all three.

| Partner | Role |
|---------|------|
| neatlogs | Trace runs; debug and compare before/after |
| Entire | Build/improve agent; checkpoints + graph (and optionally Brain) |
| cfo.ai | Business plan: pricing, costs, revenue, runway ([cfo.ai/neathacks](https://cfo.ai/neathacks); cancel subscription right after signup per FAQ) |

## Judging (weights)

| Category | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| The agent | 30% |
| Use of neatlogs, Entire, cfo.ai | 25% |
| Demo video | 20% |
| Building in public | 15% |
| Usefulness and originality | 10% |

## Evidence judges expect

- **Agent improved:** two neatlogs traces (before/after) with compared numbers  
- **neatlogs:** trace links or screenshots  
- **Entire:** checkpoints or entire-graph output in repo  
- **cfo.ai:** business plan in submission  
- **Recovery:** run where an action failed and agent continued  
- **Build in public:** links tagged **#neatHack**  
- **Code during event:** public repo with commits dated during hackathon  

## FAQs (summarized from live page, 2026-10-10)

- **Team:** 1–4 people; same team name on Luma; one team per person.  
- **Pre-build:** planning OK; **all code** between Oct 10 start and **Oct 12 deadline**; use neatlogs during development.  
- **Tracks:** one track only.  
- **Partners:** required (see above).  
- **Community winner:** $500 separate; genuine interest; bots/bought likes disqualify; partner usage not scored for this award.  
- **Conduct:** respectful; harassment/spam/plagiarism → removal.  
- **Credits:** AWS (check-in), Crustdata 5,000 for participants; Dodo Payments credits for top 3 only.
